Which areas do you serve?+

Prime AC Solution currently lists Ahmedabad as its service area. Contact us with your locality to confirm availability.

What types of AC service can I request?+

You can enquire about AC repair, servicing, installation, gas checks and refilling, uninstallation and shifting, commercial AC service, and annual maintenance.

How much does a service cost?+

Costs depend on the AC, issue and work required. Contact us with details so the scope and estimate can be discussed before work begins.

Can I book through WhatsApp?+

Yes. Fill in the enquiry form and it will prepare a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Your request is not submitted until you send that message.

Please contact the team to confirm current warranty terms, urgent availability and any applicable conditions.
